Branching logic

When the category is Internet/Wi-Fi and the issue affects 5 or more users, the ticket escalates to Level 2 within 24 hours. The form then asks one extra question: "Single or multiple users?"

IF category = Internet/Wi-Fi AND users ≥ 5
THEN escalate to Level 2 (24 h) + ask "Single or multiple users?"

B · Standard operating procedure

Ticket handling, from logging to closure

The Team Leader runs the process. Each step has a time limit, and three conditions trigger escalation.

  1. Logging 0–15 min

    The Team Leader records the issue in the system and generates a unique Incident ID.

  2. Categorization 15–30 min

    The Team Leader validates all required fields to ensure accurate classification.

  3. Level 1 resolution within 2 hours

    Initial triage uses the Knowledge Base. Password-related issues are fixed with Active Directory resets.

  4. Escalation decision

    Escalate when any of these is true:

    • The issue is unresolved after two follow-ups.
    • Network downtime exceeds 30 minutes.
    • More than five users are affected (e.g. VLAN 20).

  5. Level 2 resolution within 24 hours

    The Team Leader investigates deeper network issues: SSH access to switches, VLAN configuration, DHCP and DNS (8.8.8.8).

  6. Level 3 resolution within 48 hours or complex cases

    For advanced issues the Team Leader starts a Change Request Form and coordinates with administrators or vendors as needed.

  7. Closure

    Final testing is done, the resolution time is updated, the status is set to Resolved and a resolution email goes to the user.

Escalation to Level 2 within 24 hours if downtime exceeds 30 minutes (The Independent Institute of Education, 2026).

E · Innovation clause

PriorityPilot AI with SmartResolve

Let AI set the priority and route the ticket.

PriorityPilot AI integrates with SmartResolve to prioritize 60–80 weekly tickets by urgency and impact, then route each one to the correct level. Level 1 spends less time on complex configurations. Logging is more accurate, accountability is clearer and end users get automated emails in the TEWC5111 format. This addresses NovaTech's inefficiency in communication and links to the SLA.
